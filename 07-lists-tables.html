    07 · Lists & Tables

    Lists & Tables

    Ordered, unordered and menu lists; full table anatomy; complex headers — and why layout tables must die.

    ul, ol, menu

    <ul>
  <li>Unordered item</li>
  <li>Another item
    <ul><li>Nested item</li></ul>
  </li>
</ul>

<!-- ol attributes -->
<ol start="3">               <!-- begin at 3 -->
  <li>Third</li>
  <li>Fourth</li>
</ol>

<ol reversed>                <!-- count down -->
  <li>Bronze</li>
  <li>Silver</li>
  <li>Gold</li>
</ol>

<ol type="a">                <!-- a, A, i, I, 1 -->
  <li>Alpha</li>
  <li>Beta</li>
</ol>

<li value="10">Ten</li>     <!-- force a single item's number -->

<!-- <menu> is a semantic synonym for <ul> used for toolbars / command lists -->
<menu>
  <li><button type="button">Bold</button></li>
  <li><button type="button">Italic</button></li>
</menu>
    !

    The only permitted direct child of ul, ol and menu is <li> (plus script-supporting elements). No <div> wrappers between them.

    Full table example

    <table>
  <caption>Quarterly revenue (USD, thousands)</caption>

  <colgroup>
    <col>
    <col span="2" class="numeric">
  </colgroup>

  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th scope="col">Quarter</th>
      <th scope="col">Revenue</th>
      <th scope="col">Growth</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>

  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <th scope="row">Q1</th>
      <td>120</td>
      <td>+4%</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <th scope="row">Q2</th>
      <td>145</td>
      <td>+21%</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>

  <tfoot>
    <tr>
      <th scope="row">Total</th>
      <td>265</td>
      <td>+12%</td>
    </tr>
  </tfoot>
</table>

    Complex tables: id + headers

    <!-- When scope isn't enough (multi-level headers), link cells
     to their headers explicitly with the headers attribute. -->
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <td id="blank"></td>
      <th id="q1" colspan="2" scope="colgroup">Q1</th>
      <th id="q2" colspan="2" scope="colgroup">Q2</th>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <th id="region" scope="col">Region</th>
      <th id="q1-rev"  scope="col">Revenue</th>
      <th id="q1-cost" scope="col">Cost</th>
      <th id="q2-rev"  scope="col">Revenue</th>
      <th id="q2-cost" scope="col">Cost</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <th id="emea" scope="row">EMEA</th>
      <td headers="emea q1 q1-rev">10</td>
      <td headers="emea q1 q1-cost">4</td>
      <td headers="emea q2 q2-rev">12</td>
      <td headers="emea q2 q2-cost">5</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
    ✕

    Never use tables for layout. They are for tabular data. A layout table forces a misleading reading order on screen readers, cannot reflow responsively, and is impossible to make accessible without heroics. Use CSS Grid or Flexbox.
